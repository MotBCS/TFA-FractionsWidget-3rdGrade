Remember:The top number (numerator) tells how many pieces we have. The bottom number (denominator) tells how many pieces we have in total.

Step 2 · 1 minute 15 seconds

Learn the fraction rule

When denominators are the same, the pieces are the same size. Add the numerators and keep the denominator.

28 + 38 = 58

2 + 3 = 5 on top. The 8 stays on the bottom!
