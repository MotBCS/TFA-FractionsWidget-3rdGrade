Slide 1

What Do these Numbers Mean?

A fraction has two numbers.

Numerator - Top Number ↓
3 4
↑ Denominator - Bottom Number
The numerator (top)tells us how many pieces we are talking about.
The denominator (bottom)tells us how many total number of pieces there are.

The Fraction Rule

What happens when we add fractions with the same denominator?

3/8

5/8
28 + 38 = 58

Notice that the denominators (bottom numbers) are the same? That means we just have to add our numerators (top numbers).

"Why does the denominator stay 8? That's because the whole pizza is still cut into 8 equal pieces."
