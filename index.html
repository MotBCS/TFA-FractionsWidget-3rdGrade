Slide 1

What Do these Numbers Mean?

A fraction has two numbers.

Numerator - Top Number ↓
3 4
↑ Denominator - Bottom Number
The numerator (top)tells us how many pieces we are talking about.
The denominator (bottom)tells us how many TOTAL number of pieces there are.

Slide 2

Warm-up: Count the Pieces!

This pizza is cut into 4 equal pieces. One piece has been served.
What fraction of the pizza was served?

Think about how many pieces were served, and how many pieces are in the whole pizza.

Slide 3

Now You Try: Make 3/6!

This pizza has 6 equal slices. Tap the slices to add sauce on3 out of 6 slices.

You made: 0/6

Slide 4

What Fraction has sauce?

This pizza has 6 equal slices. Two slices have sauce.
What is the fraction?

Count the slices with sauce first, then count all the whole pizza.

Slide 5

Your Turn: Make 3/4!

This pizza has 4 equal slices. Tap the slices to add sauce until the pizza shows 3/4.

You made: 0/4

Step 6

Amazing! You're a Fraction Superstar!

Let's review what we learned.

⭐⭐⭐
1.Look at the top number.

The numerator is how many parts you have.

2.Look at the bottom number.

The denominator is how many TOTAL parts there are.
